🧠 MedAim — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

MedAim ("the App", "we", "us") is a free study companion app for NEET aspirants, providing access to PDF notes, previous year questions, and AI-generated practice questions. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses and how it is used.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

MedAim does not require account creation, login, or any personal identification to use its core features. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personal identity information through the App itself.

2. Study Content (PDFs)

PDF notes and previous year question papers are hosted on Google Drive and opened through your device's browser or Google Drive app when you tap a resource inside MedAim. MedAim does not host, store, or track which specific files you open or download; that activity happens through Google's own services and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

3. AI Question Generator

The AI Question Generator feature sends your selected subject and class level (e.g. "Biology", "Class 11") to our backend server to generate practice questions. No personal information is included in this request. Your answers during a practice test (correct/wrong/skipped) are used only to show you your results within the app and are not stored on any server or shared with third parties.

4. Advertisements

MedAim displays interstitial advertisements powered by Unity Ads to support the free availability of the app. Unity Ads may collect certain device and usage information (such as device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data) to serve and measure ads, in accordance with their own privacy practices. You can review Unity's data practices at Unity's Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions Used

  • Internet access — required to load PDFs, generate AI questions, and display ads.
  • Network state — used to check connectivity before loading content or ads.

MedAim does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or storage beyond what is needed to open downloaded files through your device's default apps.

6. Children's Privacy

MedAim is intended for students preparing for competitive exams and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not require any personal sign-up information to function.

7. Third-Party Services

MedAim relies on the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Drive (for hosting and delivering study PDFs)
  • Unity Ads (for displaying advertisements)
  • Groq API (for generating AI practice questions, accessed only through our backend — your device never contacts Groq directly)

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the App evolves. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
